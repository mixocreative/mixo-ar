How to use this

  1. 1If the URL or a local load includes more than one model, they are listed here.
  2. 2On a phone that supports it, AR places the model in the room in front of you.
  3. 3This sheet, and who made it.

Open a model from this device, or drag one onto the page. GLB, GLTF, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, STL, or a .zip holding a model with its files.

A model whose textures are separate files - an .obj with its .mtl - needs all of them, and a page cannot read a file you did not pick. Choose its whole folder under the Open button, or drag the folder onto the page.

AR does not start inside the browsers built into LINE, Facebook and other apps. Copy the link and open it in Chrome or Safari.
